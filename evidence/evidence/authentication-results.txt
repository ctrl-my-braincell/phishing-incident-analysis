## Interpretation

The available header evidence shows that SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication all passed for this message.

This provides evidence that the message was authenticated through the domains and infrastructure represented in the headers.

Authentication success does not independently establish that the message content is safe or that the sender's business intent is legitimate. Authentication primarily provides evidence about domain authorization, message signing, and DMARC alignment.

The authentication results should therefore be correlated with the message content, URL destination, redirect behavior, infrastructure, and other investigation evidence.
